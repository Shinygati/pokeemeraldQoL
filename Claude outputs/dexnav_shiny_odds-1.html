Matches ORAS 1:1 and RollDexNavShiny in src/dexnav.c. Each DexNav check succeeds with ceil(target / 100) / 10,000, where target is 6 per Search Level up to 100, then 2 per level up to 200, then 1 per level (so 1/10,000 at Search Level 1–16, up to 16/10,000 at 901+). Checks: 1, +2 with the flag, +4 when boosted, +5 on the 50th encounter and +10 on the 100th encounter only. The next encounter is the current chain + 1: every 5th encounter is boosted (when the DexNav shows 4, 9, 14 …), otherwise there's a 4% chance. Levels: +1 per 5 in the chain (back to 0 at 100) and +10 when boosted, in battle only; a caught Pokémon keeps its normal level. The chain only counts for the species it belongs to; any other species uses a chain of 0.
